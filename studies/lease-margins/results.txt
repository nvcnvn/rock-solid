   Grantor raw rho, holder's exact, margin m = the design's rule at B = rho. The

   headroom the margin leaves on a 4 s lease (real time the holder may lose unseen).
       rho       m  missed pause, threshold 1/(1-m)  with 1+ld/2  headroom
    1.0000  1.000%                         10.10 ms      5.07 ms   40.0 ms
    1.0040  1.000%                          6.14 ms      1.09 ms   24.1 ms
    1.0060  1.100%                          5.17 ms      0.00 ms   20.1 ms
    1.0101  1.510%                          5.25 ms      0.00 ms   20.4 ms
    1.0200  2.500%                          5.60 ms      0.00 ms   21.6 ms
    1.0400  4.500%                          6.90 ms      0.00 ms   26.2 ms
