== 1. Creates per second in one directory, by how shared keys are updated
   (commit latency from the design's raft rounds spike; every creator waits for its commit before the next)
   1 creator in flight, latency 0.6 ms: compare-and-set 1,667/s, Update 1,667/s; 10,000 files 6.0 s against 6.00 s
   1 creator in flight, latency 3.0 ms: compare-and-set 333/s, Update 333/s; 10,000 files 30.0 s against 30.00 s
   64 creators in flight, latency 0.6 ms: compare-and-set 1,667/s, Update 106,667/s; 10,000 files 6.0 s against 0.09 s
   64 creators in flight, latency 3.0 ms: compare-and-set 333/s, Update 21,333/s; 10,000 files 30.0 s against 0.47 s
   One loop applies about 60,000 proposals/s at 90% busy (design, Event loop per core), so the Update
   figures above about 50,000/s are capped by the shard's loop, not by conflicts.

== 2. Largest sum of grants Σ a disk's gap g_d allows, under two rules for unseen reuse
   time window: Σ + h_d <= g_d, h_d = peak rate x window;  byte budget: Σ x (1 + k) <= g_d, no h_d
   G4: 100 GB disk at 90%, 16 MiB segments, 3 GB/s, gap 9.87 GB: time window 1s 6.87, 0.25s 9.12; byte budget k=1 4.93, k=2 3.29, k=4 1.97 GB  (10 nodes x 256 MiB = 2.68 GB)
   G5: 32 GB NVMe at 90%, 4 MiB segments, 3 GB/s, gap 3.17 GB: time window 1s 0.17, 0.25s 2.42; byte budget k=1 1.58, k=2 1.06, k=4 0.63 GB  (10 nodes x 256 MiB = 2.68 GB)

== 3. Granted bytes the leader has not heard of, on one disk of a 3-node rep3 pool of G4's disks
   (every disk holds a copy of every chunk, so every granted byte can land on one disk)
   design today, one client streams 10 s at 1.1 GB/s unseen: 11.00 GB unseen + 0.81 GB granted = 11.81 GB against 9.87 GB -> overruns the log reserve
   budget k = 2, all three nodes at their budget (worst case): 1.61 GB unseen + 0.81 GB granted = 2.42 GB against 9.87 GB -> fits
   budget k = 4, all three nodes at their budget (worst case): 3.22 GB unseen + 0.81 GB granted = 4.03 GB against 9.87 GB -> fits
   cap binding, G4 with 10 nodes, k = 1: grants 4.93 GB, worst landing 9.87 GB = g_d
   cap binding, G4 with 10 nodes, k = 2: grants 3.29 GB, worst landing 9.87 GB = g_d
   cap binding, G4 with 10 nodes, k = 4: grants 1.97 GB, worst landing 9.87 GB = g_d
   Without the fixes the review found: a shrink from 256 to 50 MiB on three nodes whose replies are lost lands
   up to 2.42 GB (k = 2) while the leader counts 0.47 GB; a floor grant reused at a full disk
   lands (1 + k) x g per window, so a 1.1 GB/s writer fills G4's gap in about 9 windows.

   Share of each second a node keeps buffering with no fault, if the window resets only once a second:
   10 GbE: k=1: 24%, k=2: 49%, k=4: 98%  (grant 256 MiB)
   25 GbE: k=1: 9%, k=2: 19%, k=4: 37%  (grant 256 MiB)
   100 GbE: k=1: 2%, k=2: 5%, k=4: 10%  (grant 256 MiB)
   With an early heartbeat at half the budget, the window resets every k x g / 2 bytes, so a node at rate R
   10 GbE: 4.1 extra heartbeats per second at k = 2, and buffering never stops
   25 GbE: 10.8 extra heartbeats per second at k = 2, and buffering never stops
   100 GbE: 41.0 extra heartbeats per second at k = 2, and buffering never stops
   (one more heartbeat costs the leader a memory update, no Raft entry)
